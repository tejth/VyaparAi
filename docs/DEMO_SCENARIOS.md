# Synthetic demo data: what is inside

Everything in `data/demo_*.csv` is **synthetic** (generated by `scripts/generate_demo_data.py` with a fixed seed, so it is reproducible).
It describes a fictional Indian kirana / general store: **75 products in 11 categories, 1 Oct 2025 – 30 Sep 2026, about 41,000 sales lines, ≈ ₹1.3 crore revenue**.
Do not present any finding from it as a real business result.

Demand has weekday patterns (weekends higher) and category seasonality (summer drinks, winter tea/eggs, festive sweets and dry fruits in Oct/Nov, monsoon repellents). Stock is simulated day by day, so recorded stock reconciles with purchases and sales except where noted.

## Deliberately planted situations

| Scenario | Products / rows | Where it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| Stock-outs (supplier stopped replenishing from late August) | Aashirvaad Atta, Tata Salt, Toor Dal, Fortune Soya Oil, Maggi, Thums Up, Amul Butter*, Surf Excel* (*an open purchase order is already on its way) | Inventory alerts, recommendations, falling sales |
| Reorder needed soon | Horlicks, Haldiram Bhujia, California Almonds, Colgate, Bru Coffee, Sunfeast Dark Fantasy, Good Knight | Inventory "Reorder now" |
| Dead stock (no sale for 5+ months) | Quinoa, Chia Seeds, Extra Virgin Olive Oil | Inventory, recommendations |
| Slow-moving stock | Saffron, Walnut kernels, Imported Penne Pasta, Muesli | Inventory |
| Loss-making product (supplier cost +22% from late June, price unchanged) | Fortune Sunflower Oil 1L | Profit leakage, "losing money" question |
| Discount-driven margin erosion (30–40% discounts on many lines since July) | Haldiram Bhujia, Dettol Soap (both end up with negative recent gross margin) | Profit leakage, margin decline |
| Gradual sales decline (slide from June, drop from late July) | Parachute Coconut Oil, Lizol Floor Cleaner | Visible in the monthly trend. Not flagged as a *sudden* decline, because the last two 30-day windows are both already low |
| Sudden sales decline | The stocked-out products above | "Sudden sales decline" findings: the lost sales are the symptom of the stock-outs |
| Legitimate bulk orders (large but real) | 400 × Madhur Sugar (12 Mar), 350 × Bisleri Water (8 May) | Appear as "large quantity" for review, showing why anomalies are not proof of error |
| Excessive one-off discounts (70–90%) | 6 transactions | "Unusually large discount" |
| Quantity typing errors (×100) | 4 transactions | "Abnormally large quantity" |
| Unit price ×10 | 3 transactions | "Unexpected price change" |
| Totals that do not add up | 4 transactions | "Inconsistent total" (data quality) |
| Missing unit cost | 15 transactions | Cost estimated from inventory and flagged |
| Exact duplicate lines | 8 transactions | "Duplicate transaction", excluded from revenue |
| Customer returns (negative quantity) | 10 lines | Netted from revenue and COGS |
